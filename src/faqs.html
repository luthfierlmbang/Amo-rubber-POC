Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding specifications, ordering, certificates, and logistics for our industrial rubber sheets.

Our standard roll width is 1.0 or 1.2 meters, with standard roll lengths of 10 meters. However, depending on compound properties and volume requirements, custom widths up to 1.5 meters and specific custom cut lengths can be processed.

For standard inventory items stocked in our Bekasi warehouse, the MOQ is 1 roll. For specialized custom thickness orders or non-standard compound formulations, the MOQ typically ranges between 3 to 5 rolls per batch.

All our rubber sheets are rigorously tested. We supply SGS test reports, FDA food-grade compliance certification for hygienic applications, and certificate clearances for REACH, RoHS, and high-voltage electrical safety (IEC 61111).

Compound choice depends on environmental exposure:

• Silicone: Best for food-grade safety and extreme high-temperature resistance (-50°C to 200°C).
• NBR: Exceptional resistance to hydrocarbons, oil, gasoline, and grease.
• EPDM: Outstanding weathering, UV, ozone, and steam resistance, perfect for outdoor installations.
• Viton (FKM): Extreme chemical and acid resistance, suitable for harsh petrochemical processes.

Yes, we offer slitting services to cut rolls into narrow strips, as well as customized CNC gasket cutting. Send your CAD drawings or dimensional requirements (inner/outer diameter, bolt holes) to our sales team for an exact quotation.

Standard rolls in stock at our Bekasi HQ are shipped within 1-3 business days. For customized formulations or large manufacturing runs produced at our Chonburi factory in Thailand, delivery generally ranges from 3 to 4 weeks, including international shipping.
