Our Story

A Trusted Global Partnership

PT Abadi Makmur Optimal was established in 2017 in Jababeka, Bekasi. To guarantee the highest standards of materials, we operate in direct partnership with leading manufacturers in Thailand (Jingdong CS Rubber and AEP Venture Bangkok) located in Chonburi.

Through this partnership, we combine regional supply chain strength with world-class production facilities, ensuring our clients receive consistent quality, extensive stock options, and customized compounding services.

Production Infrastructure

Advanced Manufacturing Lines

Operating state-of-the-art machinery in Chonburi, Thailand to guarantee structural and material perfection.

2 Calendering Units

High-precision rollers that press uncured rubber compounds into sheets of uniform thickness and density. This process ensures exceptional dimensional stability for standard NBR and EPDM rolls.

4 Rotocure Units

Our continuous vulcanization process cures the rubber sheet rolls under constant heat and tension. This prevents joint lines and guarantees uniform physical properties across the entire roll length.

1 Compress Vulcanizer

Used for high-thickness plates and specialized molded components. Operates under extreme pressure and heat, critical for curing dense compounds like fluorocarbon elastomers (Viton) and thick electrical mats.
